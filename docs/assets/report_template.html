Part 1: Executive Summary & The Core Thesis

1.1 The Clinical Dilemma: High Accuracy vs. Unauditable Black Boxes

In computational epileptology, modern deep learning architectures (including Vision Transformers, Convolutional Neural Networks, and Recurrent Neural Networks) routinely achieve benchmark classification accuracies exceeding $95\%$. However, their deployment into high-stakes clinical environments—such as Intensive Care Unit (ICU) continuous telemetry and pre-surgical epileptogenic zone resection planning—has been fundamentally obstructed by the "Black-Box Interpretability Crisis."

When an automated neural network categorizes a 23.6-second intracranial EEG segment as Ictal, it is incapable of presenting an auditable neurophysiological justification. Popular post-hoc explainability techniques, such as Grad-CAM, Integrated Gradients, or Attention Weight Visualizations, have proven critically deficient in neurophysiology:

  • Unfaithfulness: Saliency maps frequently highlight high-frequency muscle artifact spikes or edge discontinuity artifacts rather than genuine paroxysmal epileptiform discharges.
  • Post-Hoc Rationalization: Saliency heatmaps indicate where an attention weight concentrated, but cannot reveal the mathematical governing rule or causal relationship driving the diagnosis.
  • Susceptibility to Confirmation Bias: Clinicians cannot determine whether the network detected true hypersynchrony or merely overfitted an uncalibrated temporal shortcut.
CORE SCIENTIFIC THESIS
A deep sequence self-attention model can match unconstrained state-of-the-art diagnostic accuracy ($98.40\%$) while routing its entire decision logic through an intrinsic, bounded layer of three biophysically grounded electrophysiological concepts ($C_1$: Paroxysmal Spike Index, $C_2$: Hypersynchronous Power Surge, $C_3$: Phase-Space Texture Disruption), producing an auditable linear decision equation with mathematically verified zero information leakage.

2.2 Multi-Mode Phase-Space Recurrence & GLCM Feature Extraction

To capture both non-stationary amplitude dynamics and non-linear phase-space trajectories, each raw signal $x(t)$ undergoes dual-stream transformation:

1. Variational Mode Decomposition (VMD): Raw signals $x(t)$ are adaptively decomposed into $K=4$ band-limited Intrinsic Mode Functions (IMFs) $u_k(t)$ by solving the constrained variational optimization problem:

$$\min_{\{u_k\}, \{\omega_k\}} \sum_{k=1}^K \left\| \partial_t \left[ \left( \delta(t) + \frac{j}{\pi t} \right) * u_k(t) \right] e^{-j \omega_k t} \right\|_2^2 \quad \text{subject to} \quad \sum_{k=1}^K u_k(t) = x(t)$$
(1)

2. Generalized Recurrence Plot (GRP) Discretization: For each mode $k \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$, phase-space trajectories are reconstructed via Takens' delay embedding and quantized into $L=64$ uniform quantile bins, guaranteeing amplitude scale-invariance.

3. Multi-Mode GLCM Tensor: Spatial co-occurrence across displacement $\mathbf{d} = (\Delta x, \Delta y) = (1, 0)$ yields the joint transition probability matrix:

$$P_k(i, j) = \frac{1}{Z} \sum_{x=1}^{M-\Delta x} \sum_{y=1}^{M-\Delta y} \mathbb{I}\left( \mathbf{R}_k(x, y) = i \ \wedge \ \mathbf{R}_k(x+\Delta x, y+\Delta y) = j \right)$$
(2)

Stacking across the 4 VMD modes yields the 4-channel tensor $\mathbf{X}_{\text{GLCM}} \in \mathbb{R}^{4 \times 64 \times 64}$.

4. Domain Cheatsheet Vector: Concurrently, a 67-dimensional electrophysiological vector $\mathbf{x}_{\text{cheat}} \in \mathbb{R}^{67}$ is computed, capturing statistical moments (standard deviation, skewness, kurtosis), Katz fractal dimension, line length, sub-band spectral powers ($\delta, \theta, \alpha, \beta, \gamma$), spectral edge frequencies ($SEF_{90}, SEF_{95}$), and non-linear entropies (sample entropy, Hjorth mobility/complexity).

2.3 Leakage-Free Stratified Normalization Protocol

LEAKAGE-FREE PROTOCOL GUARANTEE
In biomedical machine learning, fitting scalers or min-max bounds over the entire cohort before fold partitioning leaks out-of-fold distribution parameters into training gradients. In our pipeline, all StandardScaler moments ($\mu, \sigma$) and concept percentiles ($S_{p_1}, S_{p_{99}}$) are computed EXCLUSIVELY on the active sub-training partition (90% of development split). The test fold ($N=100$) remains 100% unseen until frozen inference.
$$c_k^*(i) = \text{clip}\left( \frac{s_k(i) - S_{p_1}^{(k)}}{S_{p_{99}}^{(k)} - S_{p_1}^{(k)}}, 0.0, 1.0 \right)$$
(3)

Part 3: Baseline Transformer Backbone Architecture

Before introducing the clinical concept bottleneck, we developed and validated the baseline Bonn 1D Gated GLCM Transformer to establish the maximum unconstrained performance ceiling of the architecture.

3.1 Spatio-Temporal Tokenization & Projection

The multi-mode GLCM tensor $\mathbf{X}_{\text{GLCM}} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 4 \times 64 \times 64}$ is permuted to treat each quantile energy state as an individual topological token in $\mathbb{R}^{B \times 64 \times 256}$. A linear projection maps this into model dimension $d_{\text{model}} = 32$, followed by learned 1D positional embeddings $\mathbf{P}_{\text{emb}} \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times 64 \times 32}$:

$$\mathbf{H}_0 = \text{Linear}_{256 \to 32}\left(\text{permute}_{0,2,1,3}(\mathbf{X}_{\text{GLCM}})\right) + \mathbf{P}_{\text{emb}} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 64 \times 32}$$
(4)

3.2 Pre-LN Multi-Head Self-Attention Encoder

The token sequence is processed through an $L=2$ layer Pre-LayerNorm Transformer Encoder:

$$\mathbf{Z}_l' = \text{MHA}(\text{LayerNorm}(\mathbf{H}_{l-1})) + \mathbf{H}_{l-1}, \quad \mathbf{H}_l = \text{FFN}(\text{LayerNorm}(\mathbf{Z}_l')) + \mathbf{Z}_l'$$
(5)

Multi-head attention uses $n_{\text{head}} = 4$ parallel heads ($d_k = 8$), FFN expansion dimension $d_{\text{ff}} = 64$, GELU activations, and dropout $p = 0.2$. Sequence tokens are pooled via mean aggregation and LayerNorm to produce texture representation $\mathbf{h}_{\text{tex}} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 32}$:

$$\mathbf{h}_{\text{tex}} = \text{LayerNorm}\left( \frac{1}{64} \sum_{i=1}^{64} \mathbf{H}_2[:, i, :] \right) \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 32}$$
(6)

3.3 Cheatsheet MLP & Gated Late Fusion Module

The 67-dimensional feature vector is projected through a Cheatsheet MLP to yield $\mathbf{h}_{\text{cheat}} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 32}$. A feature-wise gating unit computes dynamically weighted late fusion:

$$\mathbf{g} = \sigma\left( \text{Linear}_{64 \to 32}([\mathbf{h}_{\text{tex}} \, \| \, \mathbf{h}_{\text{cheat}}]) \right), \quad \mathbf{h}_{\text{fused}} = \mathbf{g} \odot \mathbf{h}_{\text{tex}} + (\mathbf{1} - \mathbf{g}) \odot \mathbf{h}_{\text{cheat}} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 32}$$
(7)

3.4 The 98.40% Black-Box Empirical Baseline

When $\mathbf{h}_{\text{fused}}$ is connected directly to an unconstrained classification head ($\text{Linear}_{32 \to 16} \to \text{GELU} \to \text{Linear}_{16 \to 2}$), the model achieves a pooled 5-fold accuracy of 98.40% (492/500 correct classifications) and 98.00% Seizure Sensitivity (2 missed seizures). This 98.40% benchmark established our target ceiling: the interpretable concept bottleneck must match this high-capacity accuracy.

Part 4: Intrinsic Concept Bottleneck Layer (XAI Design)

To replace post-hoc attention heatmaps with an intrinsically auditable mechanism, the unconstrained classification head was replaced by a 3-neuron Concept Bottleneck Layer connected to an interpretable linear decision head.

4.1 Mathematical Grounding of the 3 Clinical Biomarkers

Concept Biomarker Clinical Electrophysiology Deterministic Signal Formula Biophysical Rationale
$C_1$: Paroxysmal Spike Index (PSI) Spike & Sharp Wave Discharges $s_1 = \log\left( \sum_{n=1}^{N-1} |x[n] - x[n-1]| \right)$ Quantifies high-slope paroxysmal transitions and sharp wave bursts.
$C_2$: Hypersynchronous Power Surge (HPS) Ictal Rhythmic Entrainment $s_2 = \log\left( \sum_{n=1}^N x[n]^2 \right)$ Measures massive low-frequency rhythmic power and synchronous recruitment.
$C_3$: Phase-Space Disruption (PRD) Recurrence Trajectory Collapse $s_3 = \log\left( 1 + \sum_{i=0}^{63} \sum_{j=0}^{63} (i - j)^2 \cdot P_0(i, j) \right)$ Quantifies divergence of phase-space orbits and recurrence breakdown.

4.2 End-to-End Joint CBM Formulation

In the Joint CBM model, the fused latent state $\mathbf{h}_{\text{fused}}$ is projected onto the 3 clinical concepts via Sigmoid activation:

$$\hat{\mathbf{C}} = \sigma\left( \text{Linear}_{32 \to 3}(\mathbf{h}_{\text{fused}}) \right) \in [0, 1]^3, \quad \mathbf{z}_{\text{concept}} = \mathbf{W}_{\text{concept}} \hat{\mathbf{C}} + \mathbf{b}_{\text{concept}}$$
(8)

To eliminate the classic concept bottleneck penalty, a physical residual shortcut connects 3 raw metrics ($\mathbf{x}_{\text{res}} = [\log(\sigma_{\text{raw}}), \log(\text{Energy}), \log(\text{Line Length})]$) to the logits:

$$\mathbf{z}_{\text{final}} = \mathbf{z}_{\text{concept}} + \mathbf{W}_{\text{res}} \mathbf{x}_{\text{res}} + \mathbf{b}_{\text{res}}$$
(9)
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}}(\mathbf{z}_{\text{final}}, y) + \lambda \sum_{k=1}^3 \mathcal{L}_{\text{BCE}}(\hat{C}_k, C_k^*) + \mathcal{L}_{\text{sign}} \quad (\lambda = 1.0)$$
(10)

4.3 Physiological Sign Constraints on Decision Logits

PHYSIOLOGICAL MONOTONICITY CONSTRAINT
In clinical neurology, higher paroxysmal spiking ($C_1$), higher hypersynchronous power ($C_2$), and higher recurrence disruption ($C_3$) must monotonically increase seizure likelihood. A negative weight for any concept represents an unphysiological model defect. We enforced a one-sided ReLU penalty on ictal weights: $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{sign}} = \lambda_{\text{sign}} \sum_{k=1}^3 \text{ReLU}\left( -W_{\text{concept}}[1, k] \right) \quad (\lambda_{\text{sign}} = 3.0)$$

4.4 The Decoupled Sequential Architecture (S-CBM)

To decisively settle the fundamental peer-review critique of "Concept Leakage via Backpropagation" (the critique that task loss gradients flowing back into the concept bottleneck enable the network to bypass the bottleneck), we formulated the Sequential CBM (S-CBM):

  • Stage 1 (Pure Concept Estimation): The Transformer backbone is trained strictly on $\mathcal{L}_{\text{concept}}$ with ZERO task loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{task}} = 0$). The network never observes seizure labels during representation learning.
  • Stage 2 (Strict Freezing & Linear Classification): All 31,851 backbone parameters are frozen (requires_grad = False). A linear decision head ($\mathbf{W} \in \mathbb{R}^{2 \times 3}, \mathbf{b} \in \mathbb{R}^2$) is trained on predicted concepts $\hat{\mathbf{C}}$. Zero gradients can flow backward into the backbone.

In earlier prototypes, $C_2$ was formulated using a relative $\theta/\delta$ ratio, which caused severe negative drift ($r = -0.106$) due to high baseline hippocampal theta oscillations in interictal subsets C and D. All three concepts achieved high Pearson fidelity: $C_1: r = +0.9606$ ($\text{MAE} = 0.0543$), $C_2: r = +0.8834$ ($\text{MAE} = 0.0715$), $C_3: r = +0.9336$ ($\text{MAE} = 0.0676$).

Part 6: Clinical Discussion & Conclusion
